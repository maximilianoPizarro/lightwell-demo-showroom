= 5.3 Disconnected verify
:description: Practice key-based verify without running a physical air gap

On this page you name the disconnected verify procedure. This hub stays on the network. You do not demonstrate a data diode or USB drop during the ten-minute walk.

== Why this control exists

Some customer environments verify signatures without reaching the public Rekor or Fulcio endpoints. A key-based `cosign verify --key` flow is the procedure you rehearse offline. The cluster for this demo remains networked.

== Learning objectives

* Distinguish keyless verify (live on this hub) from key-based verify (named procedure)
* Avoid claiming a physical air gap on this environment
